Define a kit on the product — live price, cost & margin and the component breakdown, with optional / choice-group / min-max settings per component.

Live price, cost & margin — never stale

Price and cost are the live sum of the current component values, so when a component cost moves — e.g. shifting import freight — the kit follows automatically. A live margin (price − cost) shows on the product and on every quote line.

Two pricing modes

Sum of components — the combo price is the live total of its parts.
Fixed combo price — sell at one price; components stay informational.

Configurable combos

Mark components optional (the rep includes or excludes them) or group alternatives into a choice group to pick one — e.g. a colour or size. Adding the combo drops in the required parts, the default optionals and each group's default; a second pick from the same group is blocked.

Quantity guardrails

Give any component a min / max quantity per combo. A quote that goes outside the range is blocked with a clear message — and scaling the whole combo never trips it, because the limit is per single combo.

Colour-coded combos, drag to build

Each combo on the quotation gets its own colour, so combos are obvious at a glance. Drag a product under a combo to add it; drag it out to remove it — live, no save needed.

Discount the whole combo

Put one discount on the combo line and it applies to the entire combo — spread across every component in sum pricing, or on the combo price in fixed pricing. It prints on the collapsed combo line too.

Two colour-coded combos on one quotation. The kit line shows the kit total; the order total never double-counts.

Print your way

One tick on the quotation — Show full combo on print — prints the full breakdown of every combo, or just the combo line. Applies to the PDF, email and customer portal.

Totals you can trust

The combo total, the printed section subtotals and the order total always reconcile — collapsed or detailed, with taxes and discounts — so a customer quote is never quietly wrong.

Buys & stocks as components

Backed by a generated phantom Bill of Materials: buying the kit receives its components into stock and values them at their own cost — not as a single opaque product.

See your margin per quote

Optional Combo Cost / Margin / Margin % columns put the per-deal margin in front of the rep — and the margin follows any combo discount. Internal only: never printed on the customer document.
