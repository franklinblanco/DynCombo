DynCombo — Dynamic Kits & Combos

Bundles that price themselves — live cost & price, colour-coded on the quote, drag products in/out, and exploded into components on purchase and in stock.

Introductory launch price — going up to $15 after launch.

Native Odoo kits freeze: change a component's cost and the kit doesn't follow. DynCombo kits never go stale — their price and cost are recomputed from the current components every time. On a quotation a combo is a colour-coded group you can build by dragging products in and out; on purchase and in inventory it is recognised as its components, not one opaque product.

Define a kit on the product — live price & cost, the auto-generated Kit BoM, and the component breakdown.

Live price & cost — never stale

The combo price and cost are the live sum of the current component values. When a component cost moves — e.g. shifting import freight — the kit follows automatically. No re-editing the kit by hand.

Colour-coded combos, drag to build

Each combo on the quotation gets its own colour, so combos are obvious at a glance. Drag a product under a combo to add it; drag it out to remove it — live, no save needed.

Two colour-coded combos on one quotation. The kit line shows the kit total; the order total never double-counts.

Buys & stocks as components

Backed by a generated phantom Bill of Materials: buying the kit receives its components into stock and values them at their own cost — not as a single opaque product.

Kit total on the line

The kit header shows the kit's total (the sum of its components) while the components stay itemised below — and the order total is never doubled.

Print your way

One tick on the quotation — Show full combo on print — prints the full breakdown of every combo, or just the combo line. Applies to the PDF, email and customer portal.

Two pricing modes

Sum of components — the combo price is the live total of its parts.
Fixed combo price — sell at one price; components stay informational.

Clean & native

Built on standard sales order lines and Odoo's proven phantom BoM — taxes, discounts, pricelists, delivery, purchase and reporting all keep working. Depends on the standard mrp, sale_mrp and purchase_mrp modules. Available in English and Spanish. For Odoo 18 on Odoo.sh or self-hosted / on-premise.
