Native Odoo kits freeze: change a component's cost and the kit doesn't follow. DynCombo kits never go stale — price, cost and margin are recomputed from the current components every time. On a quotation a combo is a colour-coded group you build and configure per deal; on purchase and in inventory it is recognised as its components, not one opaque product.

Everything DynCombo does

  • Live price & cost — recomputed from the current components, never frozen.
  • Live margin — price minus cost, on the product and per quotation (rep-facing, never on the customer PDF).
  • Two pricing modes — sum of components, or one fixed combo price.
  • Build by dragging products in and out; each combo is colour-coded on the quote.
  • Move components between combos — drag a line from one combo into another and it is instantly re-coloured and re-priced.
  • Configurable combos — optional components and pick-one choice groups (e.g. colour or size).
  • Quantity guardrails — min/max allowed per component, enforced per combo.
  • Combo-wide discount — one discount on the combo applies to the whole thing.
  • Print collapsed or in detail — with correct section subtotals, taxes and discounts every time.
  • Buys & stocks as components via a generated phantom Bill of Materials.
  • 5 languages and built on standard sale lines — taxes, pricelists, delivery and reporting all keep working.

Live price, cost & margin — never stale

Price and cost are the live sum of the current component values, so when a component cost moves — e.g. shifting import freight — the kit follows automatically. A live margin (price − cost) shows on the product and on every quote line.

Two pricing modes

Sum of components — the combo price is the live total of its parts.
Fixed combo price — sell at one price; components stay informational.

Configurable combos

Mark components optional (the rep includes or excludes them) or group alternatives into a choice group to pick one — e.g. a colour or size. Adding the combo drops in the required parts, the default optionals and each group's default; a second pick from the same group is blocked.

Quantity guardrails

Give any component a min / max quantity per combo. A quote that goes outside the range is blocked with a clear message — and scaling the whole combo never trips it, because the limit is per single combo.

Colour-coded combos, drag to build

Each combo on the quotation gets its own colour, so combos are obvious at a glance. Drag a product under a combo to add it; drag it out to remove it; or drag a component from one combo straight into another — live, no save needed, re-coloured and re-priced on drop.

Discount the whole combo

Put one discount on the combo line and it applies to the entire combo — spread across every component in sum pricing, or on the combo price in fixed pricing. It prints on the collapsed combo line too.

Print your way

One tick on the quotation — Show full combo on print — prints the full breakdown of every combo, or just the combo line. Applies to the PDF, email and customer portal.

Buys & stocks as components

Backed by a generated phantom Bill of Materials: buying the kit receives its components into stock and values them at their own cost — not as a single opaque product.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to recreate my products?
No. Any existing product becomes a combo by ticking Dynamic Combo and listing its component products — nothing is migrated or duplicated.

Will the combo price update when a component's price or cost changes?
Yes. Price, cost and margin are recomputed live from the current components every time — they never freeze the way native Odoo kits do.

Can my customer see the margin?
Never. The Combo Cost / Margin / Margin % columns are internal, rep-facing only, and are not shown on the quotation PDF, email or portal.

How does it print — one line or the full breakdown?
Your choice, with a single tick on the quotation. Collapsed shows just the combo line; full shows every component. Section subtotals, taxes and discounts always reconcile with the order total.

Does purchasing and inventory work?
Yes. A generated phantom Bill of Materials explodes the kit into its components on purchase receipt and in stock, valued at their own cost — not as one opaque product.

Does it change my standard sales workflow?
No. Combos are built on standard sale order lines, so taxes, pricelists, discounts, delivery and reporting all keep working as usual.

Which Odoo versions and editions are supported?
Built for Odoo 18.0 (17.0 and 16.0 on the way), Community or Enterprise, on Odoo.sh or self-hosted / on-premise. As a code module it is not for Odoo Online (SaaS). Depends on the standard mrp, sale_mrp and purchase_mrp modules.

What languages are included?
English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and French out of the box. Need another language? Just ask.
